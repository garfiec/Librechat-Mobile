Live demo

Try Switchboard in your browser

This is the app's real interface, built from the same code as the Android and iPhone apps. Switch between Material and Liquid Glass, pick an accent color and watch the whole app follow.

Full screen

The demo is a one-time download of about 7 MB.

Needs a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

What you can try

  • Search everything. Tap the search button in the sidebar and type chocolate: matching messages show up under the chats, with the words marked. "See all" opens the full search, where tapping a result opens the chat at that line.
  • Search inside the model's thinking. Try riddle, which only appears in a reply's reasoning.
  • Search with patterns. In the full search, turn on .* and type \d+ minutes.
  • Compare two models. Open a chat's ⋮ menu, choose Compare Models, and ask whether to walk or drive to a car wash 50 meters away. The two models don't agree.
  • Make it yours. In Settings, switch between Material and Liquid Glass, light and dark, and pick an accent color.
  • Switch accounts. Tap your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to move between a personal, a work and a family server.
  • Try other screen sizes. Switch to Foldable or Tablet above the demo. Swipe the sidebar away, or bring it back with the menu button.

What it isn't

The demo runs with sample content and never connects to a server. To chat with your own models, get the app.
