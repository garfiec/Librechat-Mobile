Small-screen fixes, before and after

Each finding has two pairs. The trigger pair renders the screen at the small size, large font or locale where it broke, and should show the fix. The control pair renders the same screen on an ordinary phone (360×640dp, 1.0x, English) and should look the same before and after; it checks the fix didn't change normal phones. A render is one still frame from the top: where a fix makes content scroll, the after frame can show less than the before, so those cases add a scrolled-to-the-end frame. Tap a pair to compare it full size; in flip mode, tap to switch between before and after.
