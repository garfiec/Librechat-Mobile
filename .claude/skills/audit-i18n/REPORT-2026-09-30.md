## 7. Known blind spots

- **Placeholder and format specifiers are not checked.** A mismatched or dropped `%1$s` passes every detector, and is the most likely cause of a runtime crash in translated copy.
- **Translation quality is invisible.** Any non-ASCII text counts as translated. Stubs are detected only in `ar, ja, ko, ru, zh`, so English left in place in `de, es, fr, pt` goes undetected.
- **The shared-literal auto-exemption can hide skipped sections** (see §4). Its 280 prefetch-feature rows (275 `prefetch_*` + 5 `section_prefetch`, in the non-Latin locales only) are outside every total in this report.
- **`[H]` scans `.kt` only, and not inside `"""…"""` raw strings.** The script names `ArtifactPanel.kt`, `ArtifactWebContent.kt`, `MermaidWebContent.kt`, `MermaidDiagram.kt` and `PlatformMediaComponents.ios.kt` as verified live misses. `android:label` in `AndroidManifest.xml` is out of scope.
