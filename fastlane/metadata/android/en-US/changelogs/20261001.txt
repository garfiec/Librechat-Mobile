Settings can now check GitHub for a newer release, and What's new shows its notes in the
app. The check is off until you turn it on; once on, a one-time notice also appears above
the composer when a release ships.

Appearance offers a choice of interface style: Material or the new Liquid Glass. Android 13
and later draw the glass in full; older versions fall back to a blur or a flat surface.

Pause panels show page dots on phones and swipe between pages on tablets.
